🔢 Number Lab & HCF × LCM Balance Scale

Prime & Composite

Prime: Exactly 2 factors (1 & itself). Ex: 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19.
2 is the ONLY even prime! 1 is neither prime nor composite. 9 is the smallest odd composite.

Special Pairs & Perfect

Twin Primes: Primes differing by 2 → (3,5), (5,7), (11,13).
Prime Triplet: Only (3, 5, 7).
Perfect Number: Sum of factors = 2×number. Ex: 6 (1+2+3+6=12).

Coprimes & Golden Rule

Coprimes: Only common factor is 1 (HCF = 1). Ex: (8,15), (13,15).
Golden Balance:
HCF × LCM = Number 1 × Number 2

4 Super Fraction Rules
  • Proper vs Improper: Proper has top < bottom (3/5). Improper has top ≥ bottom (14/3 = 4 2/3).
  • Mixed → Improper: (Whole × Bottom + Top) / Bottom. Ex: 4 1/2 = (4×2+1)/2 = 9/2.
  • Simplest Form: Divide top & bottom by their HCF. Ex: 48/54 ÷ 6 = 8/9.
  • Division (Keep-Change-Flip): 5 ÷ 1/3 = 5 × 3/1 = 15. (Reciprocal of 0 does NOT exist!)

⌨️ Input Devices (Send data IN)

🧠 Processing (CPU = Brain)

🖨️ Output Devices (Show result OUT)

💾 Memory Ladder (RAM vs ROM & Storage Units)

⚡ RAM (Random Access Memory): Temporary / Volatile memory. Loses data when power goes off!

🔒 ROM (Read Only Memory): Permanent / Non-volatile memory. Stores startup instructions.

💿 Secondary Storage: Hard Disk, SSD, Pen Drive, CD/DVD, Cloud Storage.

Smallest → Largest Storage Units:
1 Bit (0 or 1) → 1 Byte = 8 Bits → 1 KB = 1024 B → 1 MB = 1024 KB → 1 GB = 1024 MB → 1 TB = 1024 GB
