Watch Out! Common Exam Traps

1. Forward = Multiply, NOT Divide!

Convert 150 m into dm:

150 ÷ 10 = 15 dm ✅ 150 × 10 = 1500 dm
2. Pad with Zeros in Columns!

Writing 40 kg 85 g in kg/g columns:

40 kg 850 g ✅ 40 kg 085 g
3. Count Every Single Step!

Convert 4200 mL into cL (only 1 step back!):

4200 ÷ 100 = 42 cL ✅ 4200 ÷ 10 = 420 cL
4. Never Mix Different Quantities!

Can you add 5 kg + 3 L?

✅ NO! kg measures Weight (mass) and L measures Capacity (liquid). They cannot be added!
